How the rules work

Pending tasks due in 2 days or less go first. Those due in 3–5 days go next. Tasks due after 5 days go later. Within each group, earlier deadlines lead, followed by type priority, difficulty, and duration.

Prolog places unfinished work into vacant periods between your classes, within your planning hours and before the deadline. These are suggestions; mark a task complete when all its work is done.
